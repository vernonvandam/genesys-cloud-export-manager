# Exports and history

The workspace includes areas for organizing resources and reviewing export
activity. Export execution is not connected yet.

## Sample information

- Overview figures are sample data and do not represent live export activity.
- Export history records shown in the app are sample records.
- The Resources page is the workspace for configuration resources; available
  export capabilities may depend on the current app version.

Do not treat the sample dashboard figures or history records as evidence that
an export has run or that a backup has been created.
